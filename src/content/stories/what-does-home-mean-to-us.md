**This is placeholder text for a sample story.** It shows how a collaborative, six-country video piece will be laid out.

We each had one week and one question: *what makes somewhere feel like home?* Nobody was allowed to film a flag, a landmark or anything from a tourist brochure. We wanted the small, ordinary things.

## How we made it

Every contributor filmed two minutes on a phone. We met online twice, across five time zones, to choose the clips. Arguing over the edit was half the fun.

## What we noticed

Kitchens appeared in four of the six films. Sounds mattered more than sights. And the two of us who have moved countries both talked about home in the plural.
