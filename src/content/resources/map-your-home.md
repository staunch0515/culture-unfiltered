## You will need

Paper, coloured pens, and the video *What Does Home Mean to Us?*

## Steps

1. Watch the video together.
2. Each student draws their home as a map of sounds, smells and people, not rooms.
3. Swap maps with a partner and ask three questions about theirs.
4. Discuss: what was similar, and what surprised you?
